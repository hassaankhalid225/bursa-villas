From brief to keys

How we work with you

Four stages, in this order, with your consultant inspecting each one.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We listen to your brief, review your plot and drawings, and explain what is realistic for your budget.

  2. 02

    Estimate and contract

    You receive an itemised estimate. Once agreed, it becomes the contract with a clear payment schedule.

  3. 03

    Construction

    Structure, envelope and finishing, with your consultant inspecting each stage.

  4. 04

    Handover

    A full snag walk-through, then keys, manuals and warranties.
