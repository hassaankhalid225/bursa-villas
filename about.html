How we work with you

Consultation. We listen to your brief, review your plot and drawings, and explain what is realistic for your budget.

Estimate and contract. You receive an itemised estimate. Once agreed, it becomes the contract with a clear payment schedule.

Construction. Structure, envelope and finishing, with your consultant inspecting each stage.

Handover. A full snag walk-through, then keys, manuals and warranties.
